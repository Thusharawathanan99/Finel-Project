# Proposal and completion-checklist traceability

Revision: 10 October 2026, Asia/Colombo. The supplied proposal and Tamil checklist are requirements to assess, not evidence that a feature works or permission to collect participant data. Read the [completion status](completion-status.md) for the final verification record and [evidence register](evidence-register.md) for research/operational work still required.

**Implemented** means code and controls exist. **Software verified** means the retained checks passed on constructed inputs or an explicitly described integration environment. **External validation pending** means accounts, hardware, licensed data, participants or institutional decisions are needed. No row below establishes clinical accuracy or complete proposal fulfillment.

## Original research questions

| Proposal question | Evaluation needed | Current answer supported by evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How accurately can computer vision extract and interpret handwritten/printed prescriptions, including medicine/dose schedules? | Independently annotated documents; field exact match, medicine/dose/time errors, review burden and retained evidence | Extraction/review pipeline exists; accuracy is unknown without a held-out document set. |
| Which OCR/NLP methods best handle diverse handwriting and Tamil/Sinhala/English? | Same language/writer-disjoint test documents, frozen comparators, equal annotation and subgroup counts | Donut/cloud paths and review controls are implemented; superiority or language coverage is not established. |
| What challenges arise when integrating the prescription reader and real-time alerts in underfunded care facilities? | Target-hardware resources, connectivity/consent gaps, cost, delivery/response latency, interviews and failures | Configurable edge/API/push integration exists; actual facility feasibility is pending. |
| Does prescription, dietary and emotional support improve adherence and overall care? | Approved baseline comparison, adherence denominators, usability/comfort, staff feedback and uncertainty | Modules exist. No causal or clinical improvement is established by software checks. |

The objectives concerning expression support, prescription reminders, personalized dietary notifications and caregiver responsiveness must be assessed separately. Motion-monitoring additions support care coordination; they do not replace the original prescription and multilingual evaluation.
