## Closing the research gaps

1. Supervisor completes protocol/safety/recruitment/sample rationale and obtains the actual university/site decision. Independently review translations and staff workflows.
2. Operator installs reviewed runtimes/models, real identities, intended EAS credentials and HTTPS. Run artificial-record camera/mobile/network/privacy tests and retain device-specific evidence.
3. Research team approves dataset agreements and independent labels/splits. Freeze models/configs before held-out tests; retain unsuccessful experiments as well as successes.
4. Approved study team collects voluntary feedback/observations, completes UAT/baseline comparisons and reports uncertainty, harms, false/missed events and missing data.
5. Reviewer fills the final report's result tables with those actual evidence IDs and checks each [proposal item/question](proposal-traceability.md). Do not replace pending fields with expected percentages.

Credentials, raw clinical records, identifiable imagery, signed forms and private backup contents belong in restricted storage. The public report may reference a custody record without revealing its contents. A software verification timestamp is not an ethics approval number, a participant count or a phone-delivery receipt.
