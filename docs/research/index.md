## Reading the status correctly

- **Implemented** means source exists; it does not mean it was tested on actual older residents.
- **Software verified** needs the exact command, commit, timestamp and retained output.
- **Operationally validated** needs real hardware/accounts/network evidence.
- **Research validated** needs approved participant/data provenance, independent labels, a frozen test split and measured outcomes.

The pose worker uses experimental temporal rules by default. Its optional logistic-regression activity trainer has no bundled trained weights. Resting is observable stillness while lying; sleep cannot be established from this pipeline. Geometric person tracking is anonymous and does not establish identity. Facial-expression observations do not establish emotions, disease, pain or medical emergencies.

The current baseline is configured by care staff, not learned automatically from resident history. Continuous cross-camera identity, continuous facial-expression-to-critical-alert inference, full physiological monitoring, SMS/Telegram delivery and automatic emergency calls are not claimed. Emergency assistance remains a human decision. The proposal's older bibliography contains unverified placeholder links; use the checked literature review in this dossier.

## Documents requiring completion before participant use

Fill the institutional investigator/complaint contacts, approved retention periods, study dates and ethics approval fields. Have Tamil and Sinhala forms independently reviewed with intended participants. Obtain the university decision and site permission, record signed scope choices, and run the operational checks. Record a refusal or withdrawal as carefully as a grant. Do not replace any pending result with an expected or fabricated number.
