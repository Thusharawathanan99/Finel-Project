## Information for you

We are studying whether a computer-assisted care system can help staff review prescriptions, remind people about medication, notice movement that may need attention, and respond to care requests. You are invited because you are an eligible resident, caregiver or volunteer under the approved study protocol. Participation is voluntary. Refusing or stopping will not reduce your normal care or services.

Depending on your separate choices, you may complete an interview/survey or try the prototype. A consented camera may observe ordinary walking, sitting, standing or resting in the explained area and time. A suitable adult volunteer may demonstrate supervised safe movements; older residents will not be asked to fall. The session length, number of visits and monitored area must be explained before you agree: ____________________.

The pose camera system normally processes pictures in device memory and saves numeric movement summaries, event times, a room/device assignment and review decisions. It does not normally record or upload raw video. One person in an assigned room may be associated with that room's resident; this can be wrong and staff can correct it. Multiple people may remain unknown. No face recognition is used for identity.

Optional visible-expression analysis produces uncertain observations, not proof of your feelings, pain or illness. It does not diagnose emergencies. Your own wellness report is separate. Staff must check alerts; the system can miss events or make false alerts. It does not replace staff supervision, medical advice or emergency care. Medication actions record a report of taking/skipping a dose, not proof of ingestion.

Risks include discomfort about a camera, misinterpretation, false/missed alerts and unauthorized data access. Staff can pause monitoring. Research simulations stop if you are uncomfortable or unsafe. You may benefit from trying the prototype, but benefit is not guaranteed. Any payment/expenses and funding or conflicts must be disclosed here: ____________________.

Only permitted care staff/researchers receive the data required for their role. Research reports use pseudonyms or anonymous summaries. Signed forms and the identity key are kept separately. No identifiable images or medical details will be published without additional permission. Approved retention period and storage location: ____________________. An optional transfer to a named cloud photo provider is explained separately; ordinary camera consent does not authorize that transfer.

You may refuse any scope, ask questions, skip interview/survey questions, pause a session or withdraw by telling ____________________ or using the consent controls. Withdrawal stops the affected future collection. Identifiable research data can be removed within the approved process until ____________________. Already irreversibly anonymous combined results may not be traceable. Necessary care/incident/audit records follow the disclosed institutional retention rules. You will receive a copy of this signed form.
